# Sample research session

In Pi:

/science question For these LCFRS grammar distributions, does agenda policy A reduce parsing time against policy B without changing recognition outcomes?
/science mode benchmarking

Then ask:

Design the cheapest fair experiment capable of discriminating A from B. Use short exploratory runs before larger ones. Record the exact grammar/sample generation, machine load, warmed-up runs, measurement method, and uncertainty. Report even if there is no advantage. Do not refactor unrelated modules or improve production-readiness.

When finished:

/science report
